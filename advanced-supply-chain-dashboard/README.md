# Advanced Supply Chain & Inventory Analytics Dashboard

![Dashboard preview](dashboard-preview.png)

## Executive objective
Provide operations leaders with a single decision-support view of delivery reliability, demand forecasting, supplier performance, and inventory exposure.

## Advanced capabilities
- Region selector driving headline KPIs and chart calculations
- What-if safety-stock factor that recalculates reorder points and inventory risk
- Formula-driven supplier composite scoring
- Forecast accuracy and actual-versus-forecast trend analysis
- Conditional formatting for delivery and inventory exceptions
- Top-five inventory risk ranking
- Separate shipment and inventory fact tables
- Auditable KPI definitions and scoring logic

## Deliverables
- Multi-sheet Excel analytics model
- 240-row shipment dataset and 80-SKU inventory dataset
- High-resolution executive dashboard preview
- SQL analysis for forecasting, suppliers, and exception management

## Skills demonstrated
Advanced Excel, scenario analysis, supply-chain analytics, KPI architecture, SQL, data modeling, forecasting, supplier scorecards, executive storytelling, and exception-based reporting.

> All data is synthetic and created solely for portfolio demonstration.
